# Sources, traceability, and bibliography

## Primary sources inspected

1. Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic, *Storytelling con datos: Visualización de datos para profesionales* (*Storytelling with Data*), 4th Spanish edition according to the supplied transcription cover (282 PDF pages, extractable text). Wiley / Anaya Multimedia. Original references retain page numbers from that transcription. Its original illustrations are not included in the TXT. The six lessons and ten chapters are the methodological core.
2. Brent Dykes, *Effective Data Storytelling: How to Drive Change with Data, Narrative, and Visuals*, John Wiley & Sons, ©2020 according to the supplied PDF copyright page (p. 3); ISBN 9781119615712 (hardcover), 9781119615736 (publisher PDF), 9781119615729 (EPUB). Supplied 413-page PDF and English transcription with Spanish editorial descriptions. Pages cited in new modules correspond to the 413-page PDF, not printed page numbers. The book's terminology was translated into Spanish in the original skill and synthesized as instructions, not as a full translation or official edition.

## Attribution policy

Framework definitions and names are attributed to their sources. Step-by-step procedures, rubrics, output contracts, worksheet formats, execution routes, adapted patterns, and synthetic cases are operational elaborations, not verbatim quotations or additional official methodology of the authors. Do not attribute the nine narrative application patterns in module 08 to Dykes: his nine story point types are a separate explicit classification. Do not attribute the 24 synthetic cases to an external book.

Other books mentioned during planning (*Storytelling con datos. Ejemplos prácticos*, *Antes y después*, and the 10th anniversary edition) are excluded because their text was not supplied. Do not introduce unverified pages or quotations. Figure descriptions added to Dykes's transcription are not part of his original text. If reproducing a figure is required, consult its PDF and permissions; do not claim a description is an exact reproduction.
