# 05 · Audit, templates, and delivery contracts

Operational adaptations of Knaflic's six lessons, not a rubric published by the author.

## Checklist

**Integrity (blocking):** data, units, dates, source, and population match; formulas and denominators checked; do not confuse percentages and percentage points; missing is not zero; forecasts distinguished; claims and causality fit evidence; counterevidence visible; bars/columns start at zero, non-misleading scales and periods, filters declared.

**Context:** audience, purpose, medium, and tone clear; defensible Big Idea; separate exploration and explanation. **Representation:** visual answers comparison, understandable order/scale/legend/labels; common baseline where possible. **Clutter and attention:** no 3D, borders, or purposeless noise; proximity, alignment, white space, and focus; stable coding. **Design and story:** title, text, unit, source, and annotations; legibility and redundant cues; horizontal/vertical logic; supported ending; actual inspection or declare pending.

## Diagnostic rubric (0–12)

0 = fails, 1 = partial, 2 = clear, N/A = not applicable; report actual denominator. Do not pass a critical integrity error even with a high score.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Context | No objective/audience | Implicit/incomplete | Explicit and appropriate |
| Representation | Inappropriate | Usable with effort | Fits task |
| Decluttering | Noise prevents understanding | Distractions | Only useful elements |
| Attention | Missing or misplaced focus | Weak | Unambiguous hierarchy |
| Design | Illegible/ambiguous | Friction | Legible/accessible |
| Story | Disconnected data | Conclusion without flow | Coherent and supported |

Skill-specific guidance: 0–6 major redesign; 7–9 improvements; 10–12 functional. N/A: equivalent proportion; insufficient evidence: not assessable. Do not certify or score unnecessarily.

## Templates

**Brief:** audience, decision-maker role, knowledge/objections, desired result, medium/time, tone, source/data, period/units/population/denominator, counterevidence and limits, Big Idea, final question/action, constraints.

**Visual specification:** ID and objective, title, type and rationale, data and reproducible transformation, axes/units, scale/baseline/limits, series/order, focus and color, context, labels/annotations, source/notes, accessible alternative, validation.

**Audit:** priority | observable problem | effect | change | verification. Critical: data or scale → correct against source; high: difficult comparison → test alternative; medium: weak focus → adjust; low: alignment → inspect.

**Storyboard:** page | claim title | evidence | visual | transition | notes/appendix. Start with situation/Big Idea, finding 1, finding 2, decision/conclusion; adapt page count to story, do not pad pages.

**Dashboard:** monitoring or explanation purpose, frequency, users, metrics and formulas, sources, update, unit, verified targets and alerts; group by decisions, not availability; KPI context; clear active filters; accessible detail where appropriate.

## Acceptance

An unfamiliar reader describes the message unaided, identifies support and limitations, makes the comparison without redundant codes, knows whether and how to act, or understands the neutral report. Artifact works in its destination (reading, projection, mobile, or print as appropriate).
